3 things I believe

Opinions I've earned the hard way

Seven years of client work teaches you a few things. These are the ones I'd argue about.

01

Boring tech ships faster

The best stack is the one your team can still maintain after I'm gone. Usually that's WordPress or Shopify — not whatever launched last month.

02

The handover is the product

Anyone can launch a site. Leaving behind docs, clean code and a client who isn't scared to touch it — that's the actual job.

03

Say the bad news early

Every project hits something ugly. Telling you in week one costs a conversation. Telling you in week six costs the deadline.
